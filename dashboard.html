Preventive Maintenance Policy Governance

A closed-loop multi-agent system that ingests censored maintenance data, re-estimates asset reliability under right-censoring, and recomputes the economically optimal preventive interval every cycle — balancing a calendar-based and an operating-time-based replacement policy under evolving cost and failure conditions.
